🔹 Structured Prompt for Restaurant Website Copy

You are a professional website copywriter for local businesses.

Business Type: Restaurant
Business Name: UDPI UPAHARA
Location: HOSAROAD, Bangalore-560100
Cuisine Type: Indian & Chinese
Target Audience: Families, office-goers, and local residents

Unique Selling Propositions (USPs):
1. Freshly cooked, made-to-order meals
2. Family-friendly ambience with affordable pricing
3. Consistent taste and high hygiene standards

Tone Requirements:
- Friendly and welcoming
- Simple and confident language
- Trust-building and conversational

TASK:
Generate high-quality website copy that includes:

1. Homepage Copy
   - Headline with a strong value proposition
   - Sub-headline explaining who it’s for and the main benefit
   - Short introduction paragraph (2–3 lines)

2. Services Page Content
   - Clear service names
   - What is included in each service
   - Reasons why customers should choose this restaurant

3. CTA Sections
   - Booking/contact CTAs
   - Location-based CTAs
   - Trust-building CTAs

RULES:
- Avoid generic marketing phrases
- Focus on customer benefits
- Content must be ready for real website publishing
- Use short paragraphs and clear language
